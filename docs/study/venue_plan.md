## Paper scope

The RASTI HWO Special Issue paper joins two questions: subhalo mass reach
across the declared lens sample, and PSF knowledge requirements for the
selected targets. Selection and template dependence explain the mass reach.
The known-PSF amplitude sweep and PSF knowledge-error block remain distinct.
The knowledge-error conclusions depend on the accepted campaign harvests.

The SPIE proceedings and poster are already submitted and archived in
`../spie/`. Following the author's 2026-09-04 brightness-bug ruling, cite
SPIE as framework heritage only. Do not reuse its detection, completeness,
or false-positive numbers as RASTI validation. Preserve the submitted archive.

## Agreed manuscript structure

1. Introduction.
2. Simulation suite and lens population: observing assumptions, foreground-free
   convention, source bank, parent design, and reference/selected/validation tiers.
3. Detection metrics: Fisher and nonlinear statistics, threshold conventions,
   mass ladders, aperture estimands, censoring, and validation protocol.
4. Detection sensitivity with a known PSF: reference versus selected mass reach,
   threshold dependence, measured template dependence, selector comparison,
   nonlinear transfer, and the calibrated targeted null.
5. PSF degradation and mismodeling: known-PSF amplitude sweep, knowledge-error
   area diagnostics, and nonlinear control/recovery results when accepted.
6. Discussion: mission implications within the declared assumptions, held
   claims, source/foreground limitations, and future work.
7. Conclusions, followed by the required endmatter.

The author has suggested keeping a broader morphology investigation for a
later study. The measured template dependence can remain in section 4 without
adding a new experiment or promising a general morphology result.
